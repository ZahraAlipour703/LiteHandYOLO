A lightweight single-stage hand detector for real-time robotic vision.

Abstract

Hand tracking is an important component of human-computer interaction and vision-guided robotics, where accurate and low-latency hand localization is essential for manipulation, teleoperation, and human-robot collaboration.

We present LiteHand-YOLO, a lightweight single-stage hand detector based on YOLOv8n. The architecture integrates depthwise separable convolutions and cross-stage partial connections with Efficient Channel Attention (ECA) and Coordinate Attention (CoordAtt), together with targeted detection-head refinements. These modifications are designed to improve fine-grained hand feature representation while maintaining low computational cost.

On the Hand Keypoints Dataset, LiteHand-YOLO achieves 99.26% mAP@0.5 and 91.42% mAP@0.5:0.95, improving over YOLOv8n by +0.08% and +1.30%, respectively. At the same time, the model reduces computational cost by approximately 28% to 6.4 GFLOPs and reduces the parameter count by approximately 35% to 2.06M.

On an Intel Core i7-6700 CPU without GPU acceleration, LiteHand-YOLO reaches 8.83 FPS with a mean detection confidence of 0.87, corresponding to a 6.4% speed improvement over the baseline. These results demonstrate the potential of LiteHand-YOLO for low-resource, real-time robotic hand detection.

Method

LiteHand-YOLO retains the three-part structure of YOLOv8 while introducing lightweight feature extraction, attention-based recalibration, and a refined multi-scale detection head. The design targets the fine-grained spatial structure of hands, while preserving the efficiency required for real-time inference.

YOLOv8n Baseline

YOLOv8 is a single-stage, anchor-free detector composed of a backbone, feature-aggregation neck, and detection head. The nano variant, YOLOv8n, is designed for resource-constrained environments while maintaining strong detection capability.

  • Backbone: hierarchical feature extraction using C2f blocks
  • Neck: PANet-inspired multi-scale feature aggregation
  • Head: anchor-free, decoupled detection across P3, P4, and P5

Conclusion

LiteHand-YOLO is a lightweight YOLOv8n-based hand detector incorporating Efficient Channel Attention, Coordinate Attention, lightweight convolutional operations, and detection-head refinement.

On the Hand Keypoints Dataset, the model achieves 99.26% mAP@0.5 and 91.42% mAP@0.5:0.95 while reducing computational cost to 6.4 GFLOPs and the parameter count to approximately 2.06M.

CPU-based evaluation further reports 8.83 FPS on an Intel Core i7-6700 system without GPU acceleration, supporting the use of the model in resource-constrained robotic vision applications.
